The short version

What is BETina Live?

BETina Live is the live football scores part of the BETina app. It lists the matches being played right now, keeps the score up to date while the ball is rolling and puts the teams you follow at the top. You do not have to keep the app open: when something happens in a match you care about, a notification tells you.

Most live score apps stop there. BETina Live sits inside an AI sports companion, so the score is the start of a conversation. You can ask BETina who scored, how the table looks now or what she makes of the first half, in the chat or on a voice call. Live scores are available for football; talking about other sports works in the AI sports chat.

Goal alerts

A push the moment your team scores.

Goal alerts are the reason most people install a football scores app, and they are the centre of BETina Live. Follow a team and you get a push notification when a goal is scored in its match, with the new score and the minute.

Alerts only arrive for the teams and matches you chose. Nobody needs fifty notifications from leagues they do not watch. Besides goals, BETina Live reports the other moments that change a match: kick-off, red cards, half-time and the final whistle.

Goal notifications reach you on iPhone and Android. You can switch them off again in the settings at any time.

Set-up

How to set up goal notifications for your team.

It takes about a minute, and you only do it once.

01

Get BETina

Download the app for free from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with Apple, Google, email or your phone number.

02

Pick your teams

Choose your favourite team when you start. You can follow more teams, or a single match, in the Live tab.

03

Allow notifications

Say yes when your phone asks whether BETina may send notifications. Without that, no goal alert can reach you.

04

Watch or do not

Put the phone away. The next time your team scores, you will know within the same match minute, wherever you are.

The difference

Ask about the match: live scores with an AI companion.

A score tells you what happened. It does not tell you why, and it does not care. BETina does both. She has the live data of the match in front of her, so you can ask the things you would ask a friend who is watching: who scored, whether the lead is deserved, what the result means for the table.

Every match page also carries BETina's take, a short read of the game in her own words, next to the table and the head-to-head record of the two teams. And because BETina speaks nine languages, a fan in Zagreb gets the same match in Croatian that a fan in Madrid gets in Spanish.

Questions & answers

BETina Live: frequently asked questions.

Is BETina Live free?

Yes. Live scores and goal alerts are part of the free BETina app on iPhone, Android and the web. There is no subscription.

How do I get a notification when my team scores?

Install BETina, pick your team and allow notifications. From then on you get a push whenever a goal is scored in a match of a team you follow.

Can I get goal alerts for one team only?

Yes. Alerts are sent only for the teams and single matches you follow, so one team is enough. You can add or remove teams at any time.

Which sports have live scores?

Live scores and goal alerts are available for football. You can talk with BETina about many other sports, such as tennis, NFL, MMA or cricket.

What else do I get a push for besides goals?

For kick-off, red cards, half-time and the final result of the matches you follow.

What can I ask the AI about a match?

Anything you would ask a friend who is watching: the score, who scored, how the table looks or what she thinks of the game. BETina is an AI and can be wrong, so treat her view as an opinion.

Is real money involved?

No. BETina is an entertainment app. No money is staked or paid out anywhere in the app.
