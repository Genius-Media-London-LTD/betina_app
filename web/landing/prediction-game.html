The short version

What is the BETina prediction game?

The BETina prediction game is a free football score prediction game inside the BETina app. Before a match kicks off you enter the result you expect. After the final whistle the app compares your prediction with the real score and awards points. Those points decide your place on the leaderboard, overall and in the private rounds you set up with friends.

It is the game people have played in offices and group chats for decades, minus the spreadsheet and minus the money. What is new is who you play with: BETina, the AI sports companion in the app, predicts every match too, hosts the game and is happy to argue about your 3–1.

How it works

The prediction game in three steps.

01

Predict the score

Open the match list, pick a fixture and enter the result you expect, for example 2–1. You can change your prediction as often as you like until kick-off.

02

Watch the match

At kick-off predictions are locked and revealed, so nobody can copy. If you follow the match in BETina Live, your prediction sits next to the live score.

03

Collect your points

After the final whistle your points are added automatically and the leaderboard updates. A push tells you how your matchday went.

Scoring

How points are awarded.

One prediction, three ways to score. What counts is the result after 90 minutes plus stoppage time.

3points

Exact score

You predicted the result to the goal. The best feeling in the game.

Predicted 2–1 · Result 2–1
2points

Right goal difference

You had the winner and the margin, but not the exact score. A draw with a different score counts here too.

Predicted 2–1 · Result 3–2
1point

Right winner

You called who wins, but the margin was different.

Predicted 2–1 · Result 3–0

A wrong call scores nothing and costs nothing. On top of the match predictions there are bonus questions for extra points. Every point you score also earns you XP for your level in the app.

With friends

Play with friends and climb the leaderboard.

A football prediction game is best with people you know. In BETina you create a private round, invite your friends, family or colleagues and get your own ranking, separate from the overall leaderboard. Whoever is top on Monday morning gets to talk.

The ranking shows who gained and who dropped places on the last matchday, and your personal statistics show how often you hit the exact score. There is also a team challenge: after every round the average of your group is compared with BETina's score. Beat her together.

The difference

An AI companion who plays along and talks football with you.

Every other prediction game is a form and a table. In BETina the game has a host. BETina predicts every match herself. Her predictions are hidden until kick-off, exactly like everyone else's, and she appears in every ranking, so you always have someone to beat.

And you can talk to her about it. Tell her why you see an away win, ask what she makes of a team's form, or let her tease you about last weekend. She answers in your language, by text or on a voice call. Her view is an opinion and not advice, and she gets it wrong often enough to keep things interesting.

The principle

No money, no stakes: just points and bragging rights.

The BETina prediction game is played for fun. You stake nothing, you buy nothing and nothing is paid out. There are no cash prizes and no entry fees, and there never will be a moment where the app asks for your card. What you win is points, XP, a place on the leaderboard and the right to remind your friends of it.

That is deliberate. BETina is an entertainment app for adults, and the game is meant to make a matchday more fun, whether your team plays or not.

Questions & answers

Prediction game FAQ.

Is the BETina prediction game free?

Yes. The game is part of the free BETina app on iPhone, Android and the web. There is no entry fee and no subscription.

Is real money involved, and can I win prizes?

No. You stake nothing and nothing is paid out. There are no cash or material prizes. You play for points, XP and your place on the leaderboard.

How are points scored?

Three points for the exact score, two for the right winner with the right goal difference, one for the right winner. The result after 90 minutes plus stoppage time counts.

Which leagues and competitions can I predict?

Bundesliga, Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A and the Champions League.

Can I play the prediction game with my friends?

Yes. Create a private round in the app and invite your friends. You get your own ranking in addition to the overall leaderboard.

When is the deadline for a prediction, and can I change it?

You can enter and change a prediction until the match kicks off. At kick-off it is locked, and only then can the others see it.

How is BETina different from other football predictor apps?

You play with an AI companion. BETina predicts every match herself, appears in every ranking and talks with you about your predictions, in nine languages.

Is the prediction game available on iPhone, Android and the web?

Yes. You can play in the iPhone app, the Android app and the web app in your browser, with the same account.
