Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?
With Apple, Google, your email address or your phone number — whichever is easiest for you. With email or phone we send you a one-time code.
I didn't receive my login code
Codes usually arrive within a minute. Check the email address or the number and country code, look in your spam folder, and tap “Resend code”. Still nothing? Email us and we'll help.
Can I use BETina without an account?
Yes. On the welcome screen tap “Browse first” to read live sports news and articles without signing in. An account is only needed for personalised chat and your journey.
How do I change my email address or phone number?
Email us from your registered address or with your current number and we'll move your account over safely.
How do I delete my account?
Open the app → Settings → “Delete account”. This permanently removes your profile, chat history and data. You can also email us to request deletion.
Is BETina free?
Yes. BETina is free to download and free to use on iPhone, Android and in the browser.
Is BETina a gambling app?
No. BETina is an entertainment / AI companion app for adults (18+). No real money is involved anywhere in the app. The prediction game is played for points, XP and a place on the leaderboard.
Which languages does BETina speak?
English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Romanian, Turkish and Croatian.
Which sports have live scores?
Live scores and goal alerts are available for football. You can talk with BETina about many more sports.
