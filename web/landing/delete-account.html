Account & data deletion

Delete your BETina AI account

This page explains how to delete your account in BETina AI (developer: Genius Media London Ltd) and the personal data linked to it. You can do this inside the app or without the app by email.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open BETina AI and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom tab bar).
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Without the app (by email)

Send a deletion request to marketing@geniusbet.com with the subject “Delete my BETina AI account” and the phone number you registered with, so we can find your account.

We confirm the request by email and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

  • Your login (phone number) and account
  • Your profile: name, country, birthday, language, favourite sports and team
  • XP, VIP tier and activity history
  • Your full chat history with BETina and the things BETina remembered about you
  • Reminders, notifications and your push-notification token

What is kept

Nothing from your BETina AI account is kept after deletion, except where we are legally required to retain specific records.

Your GeniusBet player account on geniusbet.com is a separate service and is not affected. To close it, please contact GeniusBet support.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can also ask us to delete specific data — for example your chat history or BETina's saved memories — without deleting your account. Email marketing@geniusbet.com with your registered phone number and what you want deleted. We handle it within 30 days.
